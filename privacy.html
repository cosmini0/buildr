Privacy Policy

What data Buildr collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over your information.

Last updated: 28 May 2026  ·  Applies to: the Buildr iOS app and buildrwork.com

Who we are

Buildr is a collaborator-matching app that helps builders, founders, creators, designers, developers, and other project people find people and projects to work with. For the purposes of UK and EU data protection law, the data controller responsible for your information is:

To run a matching and messaging service, Buildr collects the following categories of data. Most of it is provided by you when you create and use your profile.

How we use your data

  • To create your account and let you sign in.
  • To build your profile and show it to other users in the discovery feed.
  • To match you with people and projects, using signals like skill fit, interest overlap, location, and activity.
  • To run real-time chat once a match is created, including notifications about new matches and messages.
  • To manage your Buildr Pro subscription and the features it unlocks.
  • To show ads to users who are not subscribed to Buildr Pro (see Advertising & tracking below).
  • To keep Buildr safe - for example, processing reports, blocks, and content-safety checks on profiles and projects.
  • To diagnose crashes, prevent abuse, and improve the app.
  • To comply with our legal obligations.

Legal bases for processing

Under UK and EU GDPR we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Contract - to provide the core service you sign up for (your profile, matching, chat, and subscription).
  • Consent - for location access, push notifications, and tracking for personalised advertising. You can withdraw consent at any time through your device settings or in-app controls.
  • Legitimate interests - to keep the app secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and improve our service, balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligation - where we must retain or disclose data to comply with the law.

Advertising and tracking

If you do not subscribe to Buildr Pro, Buildr shows ads supplied through Google AdMob. With your permission (requested through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt), an advertising identifier and certain usage data may be used to personalise ads and measure their performance. This is considered "tracking" under Apple's rules.

You can refuse or withdraw this permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device, or by managing your consent choices in the app. Refusing tracking does not stop you seeing ads; it means they will be less personalised. Subscribing to Buildr Pro removes ads entirely.

How long we keep your data

We keep your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data, except where we need to retain limited information to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our terms. Crash and diagnostic data is kept only as long as needed to fix issues. Blocked-user and report records may be retained for safety purposes.

Your rights

Under UK and EU GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data (you can edit most of this directly in the app).
  • Delete your data - you can delete your account from within the app, which removes your profile from discovery and chats.
  • Export a copy of your data - Buildr provides an in-app data download/export option.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent (for example, location or tracking) at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, use the in-app controls or email hello@buildrwork.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk, or your local EU supervisory authority.

Security

We use the security features provided by Firebase and Apple, including encrypted connections and access controls, to protect your data. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information and to limit access to it.

Children

Buildr is not intended for anyone under 18, and you must confirm your age during sign-up. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "last updated" date above, and where changes are significant we will provide notice in the app.
